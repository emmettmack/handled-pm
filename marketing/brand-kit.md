# Macalisto brand kit

Read this before creating any content. If anything here conflicts with the live
site, the site wins - and update this file.

## Product in one line
Maintenance, handled. Tenants text one number; Macalisto reads every message,
finds the right vendor, and drafts the dispatch. The landlord just approves.

## Voice
Direct, warm, plain words. Short sentences. No hype, no buzzwords, no exclamation
marks stacked up. Talk like a founder who builds the thing, not like a brand.
It is fine to say "I" when the post is from Emmett as founder.

## Audiences, in order
1. Small independent landlords (one house to a few dozen doors), Los Angeles first.
2. Small residential property managers.
They care about time, liability, and tenant satisfaction - in that order.

## Claims you may make (each must stay true to its source)
- Tenants text one number, in their own language, with photos. No app. (site/index.html)
- Eight languages. (site/index.html)
- Macalisto triages, asks follow-up questions, and drafts the dispatch from the
  landlord's own vendor list. (README.md)
- Nothing goes to a vendor or owner until a human taps Approve. (README.md, site/)
- Emergencies are flagged instantly with safety instructions to the tenant. (site/)
- Lockouts from unknown numbers are held for identity checks. (site/)
- Owner spend limits per property; over-limit repairs get flagged. (site/)
- Vendor no-response timer re-drafts to the next vendor. (README.md)
- Free while in early access; paid plans later, flat monthly price by doors,
  no minimums, no setup fee, no contract. (site/pricing.html - check it first)
- Live in 5 to 10 minutes. (site/index.html)


## Verified vs intended (check before every claim)
As of 2026-11-06:
- VERIFIED: the public browser demo at macalisto.com (Live demo tab) works end
  to end with simulated tenant texts; the site pages describe the product as
  designed; early access is free per site/pricing.html.
- INTENDED, NOT PROVEN IN PRODUCTION: real tenant SMS over a live number.
  Twilio is not connected in the public demo and the carrier (10DLC) campaign
  approval is pending. Vendor texting is off by default (VENDOR_TEXTING).
- Content may describe the product experience the way the public site does -
  that is the designed flow and Emmett's own positioning. But never state or
  imply that real texting is live, carrier-approved, or handling real tenants
  today, and never cite usage numbers, customer results, or uptime. Until
  Emmett confirms the campaign is approved, treat "texting is live" as a claim
  he must personally sign off on, per post.

## Never
- No income claims, savings guarantees, or "never miss an emergency" promises.
- No comparisons you can't source on the spot (AppFolio pricing comparison lives
  on site/pricing.html and site/compare.html - quote those pages exactly or not at all).
- No tenant data, real addresses, or real phone numbers. Use the demo's sample
  names and places only if a post needs an example.
- No invented features. If it's not in README.md or site/, it doesn't exist.
