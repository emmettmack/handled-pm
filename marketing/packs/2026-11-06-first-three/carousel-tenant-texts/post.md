## Caption
It's 11pm. A tenant texts about a leak.

You could spend the next hour playing phone tag. Or it could go like this:

1. They text one number. Any language, photos welcome, no app.
2. Macalisto works out what's wrong, asks what's missing, and drafts the fix
   with the right vendor from your list.
3. You read a finished draft and tap Approve. Nothing goes out until you do.

That's the whole job. You're back to your evening.

Free while in early access. Link in bio to try the live demo.
