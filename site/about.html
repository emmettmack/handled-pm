About

I built this because I needed it.

I'm Emmett Mack. I'm a student at USC, I run track, and I've spent a lot of time around rental properties, from both sides of the lease. Macalisto is what I wished my landlord had.

Learning how a rental actually runs

I learned how to run rental properties by being around my family's. Nobody hands you a manual. You find out that a "small" repair is a phone call, a photo, a plumber who may or may not pick up, a tenant waiting for an answer, and a note you forgot to write down. You find out that most of the job is keeping people informed.

Then I was the tenant

As a renter, I got to see the other side. My landlord changed management companies more than once, and each change meant a new portal, a new phone number and a new way to ask for things. Repair requests got lost in the handoff. I rarely knew who to ask, whether anyone had seen my message, or when someone was coming. The repairs weren't hard. The communication was the problem.

I kept thinking: this is a text message problem. A tenant should be able to text one number, describe what's wrong, send a photo, and get a clear answer. The person in charge should be able to see everything and decide, without a stack of software built for a company with ten thousand doors.

What I'm building

Macalisto answers your tenants by text, asks the questions a vendor would ask, reads the photos they send, drafts the message to the right vendor, and waits for your approval. Nothing goes out without you. It is built for people who run a handful to a few dozen properties, which is a lot of landlords and not many software companies.

Software for big property managers is built around teams, minimums and long onboarding calls. I'd rather build something you can start using today, that costs a price you can see, and that sounds like you and not like a call center.

Why I think I can do it

Track taught me to show up early, do the boring work, and keep going when it isn't fun. I've started a business before, so I know that shipping something and answering the phone matters more than a perfect plan. And I'm a landlord's tenant and a landlord's family member, so I'm building for people whose problems I've watched up close.

You'll reach me, not a ticket queue. While Macalisto is this size, I set up new customers myself and read every message that comes in. If something doesn't work for you, tell me and I'll fix it.
