Where Macalisto fits.

Macalisto does one job well: it handles the back-and-forth when something breaks. It does not do your accounting or collect your rent. Here is how that lines up against the other options, including what Macalisto does not do.

Use Macalisto alongside what you already have

If you already collect rent through another tool, keep it. Macalisto works next to it, and takes over the part where tenants text you about a leak at 9pm. If you want one system for everything and you manage a large portfolio, AppFolio is a serious product built for that.
