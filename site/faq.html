FAQ

Straight answers.

The questions I would ask before trusting something with my tenants. If yours is not here, email me.

How does it work?

Your tenants text one number. Macalisto asks what's wrong, asks for a photo when a photo would help, works out how urgent it is, and drafts a message to the right vendor from your list. You read it and approve. Nothing is sent to a vendor until you do.

Do my tenants need an app or an account?

No. They text a phone number. They never need to know about the software.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

You see its reasoning next to every draft, and you can edit or reject it. For emergencies and sensitive cases it brings you in right away instead of deciding on its own. It never promises money or says who is at fault; those questions come straight to you.

Can it message my vendors on its own?

No. Every vendor message is a draft that you approve first.

What about emergencies?

Gas, flooding, sparks, no heat in freezing weather and similar situations are flagged the moment they arrive. The tenant gets fixed safety instructions, including calling 911 where it matters, and you see a red banner at the top.

Can I take over a conversation?

Yes. Open any conversation in your inbox and reply yourself. The assistant pauses on that thread so you are not talking over each other, and you can hand it back with one click.

Does it read photos?

Yes. It looks at the photos tenants send and uses what it sees when it asks questions and writes to your vendor. It cannot watch videos yet, and it will say so and ask the tenant to describe them.

What languages does it speak?

English, Spanish, Korean, Chinese (simplified and traditional), Armenian, Tagalog and Vietnamese. The fixed messages are translations that I am still having reviewed by native speakers.

I only have one or two properties. Is this for me?

Yes. There is no minimum and no limit at the small end. If you rent out your own home, there is a setup for that.

I manage more than 50 properties.

Macalisto may still help, but it is built for smaller portfolios. If you are on a larger system today and happy, keep it. Talk to me if you want to try it alongside.

Does it collect rent or do accounting?

No. That is deliberate. It does not replace your rent tool or your bookkeeping; it handles maintenance and the conversations around it.

Can I leave and take my data?

Yes. Your work orders, messages, photos and vendor list are yours. Right now I will send you a complete copy on request; a self-serve export is on the list.

Is my tenants' information safe?

Each account's data is kept in its own database, with daily backups. Texts go through Twilio, the AI is provided by Anthropic, sign-in by Clerk and hosting by Render. The security page lists exactly what is and is not in place.

How do tenants stop the texts?

They reply STOP. Macalisto records it and does not text them again unless they reply START.

What does it cost?

It is free during early access. See the pricing page for how paid plans will work.

Who is behind this?

One person. I'm a student and a landlord's tenant who got tired of repairs getting lost. More on the about page.
