Your maintenance line, run for you.

Handled answers tenants, sorts out what's wrong, finds the right vendor from your own list, and writes the dispatch. Your only job is to press Approve.

From text to dispatch in five steps

Tenants text one dedicated maintenance number. Everything after that is prepared before you look.

Tenant texts

"My sink is broken." In English or Spanish, from any phone. Nobody downloads an app.

Handled sorts it out

It reads the message, works out the category (plumbing, electrical, heating and cooling, appliance, lockout) and how urgent it is.

It asks what it needs

If something is unclear, it texts the tenant a natural follow-up question, at most two, then moves on with what it has.

It prepares the dispatch

A plain-English summary, the best vendor from your list and why, and a ready-to-send message with the address, tenant contact and access notes.

You approve

Approve, edit, or reject with a note. Nothing goes to a vendor until you do.

What you get

Built for companies with 10 to 300 doors, where one or two people handle everything.

An approval queue, not another dashboard

Each draft shows the summary, the AI's reasoning, the matched vendor and the message. You confirm and move on.

Your vendors, your rules

It only picks from the vendors you add, using trade, your preferred flag and your notes ("great with drain clogs", "speaks Spanish", "24/7").

Every work order in one place

Full conversation, status history from New to Closed, vendor details and your private notes, grouped by status.

Always-on tenant replies

Tenants get a warm, clear answer in seconds, day or night, in the language they wrote in.

Safety is built in, not bolted on

These are rules the product enforces, not suggestions to the AI.

Emergencies

Flagged the moment they arrive

Gas smells, flooding, sparking outlets and no heat in freezing weather get a red banner, a pinned work order and instant safety instructions to the tenant. The live product pages you by phone.

Human approval

Nothing sends without your OK

Every message to a vendor or owner is a draft until you approve it.

Liability

It never promises money or admits fault

It can say "we'll get this handled." It can't say "we'll pay for it." Requests for compensation are answered neutrally and flagged for you.

Security

Lockouts are verified first

The texter's number must match the tenant on file. If not, it asks for a name and unit, and if it still can't verify, it holds the ticket for you. Nobody is sent to open a door for a stranger.

Spending

Owner spend limits respected

If an estimate is over a property's approval limit (for example $500), you get a drafted owner approval request instead of a vendor dispatch.

Reliability

Silent vendors don't stall repairs

If a vendor hasn't replied in 15 minutes, the ticket is flagged and a dispatch to the next best vendor is drafted for you.

Questions

Do tenants know they're talking to an AI?

Tenants get a clear, friendly text thread with your maintenance line. You decide how it is introduced to them.

Can it message vendors on its own?

No. Vendor and owner messages are always drafts that you approve first.

What if it gets something wrong?

You see its reasoning next to every draft and can edit or reject it. Emergencies and sensitive cases are escalated to you rather than guessed.

How long does setup take?

Add your vendors and your properties and set each owner's spending limit. Then point your maintenance number at Handled.
