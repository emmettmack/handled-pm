From text to dispatch in five steps

Tenants text one dedicated maintenance number. Everything after that is prepared before you look.

1

Tenant texts

"My sink is broken." English or Spanish, from any phone. No app to download.

2

Handled sorts it out

It works out the category and how urgent it is, and flags emergencies instantly.

3

It asks what it needs

A natural follow-up question when something is unclear. Two at most, then it moves on.

4

It prepares the dispatch

A plain-English summary, the best vendor from your list, and a ready-to-send message.

5

You approve

Approve, edit, or reject. Nothing goes to a vendor until you do.

What lands in your queue

Not a pile of tickets to sort. A finished draft you can read in ten seconds.
