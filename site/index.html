Three steps. That's it.

Your tenants text one number. Macalisto does the legwork. You make one decision.

1

Tenant makes a request

By text or email, in English or Spanish, with photos or video if they have them. No app to download.

2

Macalisto takes it from there

It works out what's wrong and how urgent it is, asks the tenant anything it's missing, and proposes a fix with the right vendor from your list.

3

You approve

Read a finished draft, edit it if you like, and send it. Nothing goes out until you do.

Questions

Can it message vendors on its own?

No. Vendor and owner messages are always drafts that you approve first.

What if it gets something wrong?

You see its reasoning beside every draft and can edit or reject it. For emergencies and sensitive situations, Macalisto brings you in right away instead of deciding on its own.
