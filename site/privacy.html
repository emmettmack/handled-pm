Macalisto

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Macalisto, operated by Emmett Mack (Macalisto) ("Macalisto", "we"), is a maintenance coordination service for people who rent out homes. A landlord or property manager (our customer) uses Macalisto to receive repair requests by text message, and to contact repair vendors. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

From tenants: the phone number you text from, the content of your messages and any photos or videos you send, and your preferred language. From our customers: name, email address, company name, properties, units, tenant contact details they enter, and vendor contact details and rates they enter. From vendors: name, phone number, email address and messages about jobs.

Photos and videos

Photos you send are stored on our servers and shown to the AI service so it can understand the problem. Videos are stored with the work order but are not analyzed by the AI. They are not public: they are shown to the property manager through short-lived private links.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the service: to log and triage repair requests, to send replies and updates by text message, to contact vendors about jobs the property manager approved, and to keep our customers' records. We also use limited usage data to keep the service secure and working.

Text messaging

Tenants and vendors receive text messages only about repair requests, such as confirming a request, asking for details, scheduling a visit, or giving an update. Message frequency varies with the request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase or lease.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Who we share information with

Only with service providers that help us run Macalisto (for example our text messaging carrier Twilio, our hosting provider, our sign-in provider Clerk, and Anthropic, whose AI reads messages and photos and drafts replies), and with the property manager and vendors involved in your request. These providers may use the information only to provide their service to us. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Retention and security

Records are kept while the customer's account is active, and deleted or returned on the customer's request. Data is stored on access-controlled servers and transmitted over encrypted connections.

Your choices

You can opt out of texts by replying STOP. To ask us to access, correct or delete your information, contact us below; tenants may also contact their landlord or property manager, who controls the records in their account. California residents can ask what personal information we hold about them and ask us to delete it by emailing the address below.
