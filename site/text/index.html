Maintenance request line

Text your maintenance request

This is a maintenance request line operated by Macalisto on behalf of landlords and property managers. If your landlord or property manager gave you this number, text it to report a repair or ask about one that is already open.

Text this number
(805) 909-3422

On a phone, tap the number to open a text. On a computer, scan the code with your phone.

Scan to text

Text your maintenance request to (805) 909-3422. By texting, you agree to receive text messages from Macalisto about your repair request, such as confirmations, questions, scheduling and updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any lease or purchase.

By texting this number you also agree to the Terms of Service and acknowledge the Privacy Policy.

How it works

  1. You text the problem. Describe what is wrong. Photos help and can be sent in the same conversation.
  2. We ask for what a repair person needs. This may include where the problem is, how serious it is, and when someone can get into your home.
  3. Your property manager reviews and approves the repair. Nothing is sent to a repair person until they do.
  4. You are kept informed. You will get a text with the day and time once a repair person confirms, and a follow-up when the work is done.

In an emergency

If there is a fire, a gas smell, a medical emergency or immediate danger, call 911 first. This number is for maintenance requests and is not an emergency service.
