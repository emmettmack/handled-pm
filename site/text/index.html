How it works

  1. Text the problem to the number above.
  2. Macalisto confirms your request and may ask a few questions.
  3. Your property manager approves the repair.
  4. You get updates by text until it’s done.

In an emergency, such as fire, gas or a medical emergency, call 911 first.
